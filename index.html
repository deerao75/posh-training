ABOUT THIS TRAINING

What the course covers

What POSH is

The law is the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013, and the Rules made under it. Section 2(n) defines sexual harassment as any unwelcome act or behaviour of a sexual nature, directly or by implication. That includes physical contact and advances, a demand or request for a sexual favour, a sexually coloured remark, showing pornography, and other unwelcome physical, verbal, or non-verbal conduct of a sexual nature.

Why it applies

The Act applies to every workplace in India. A workplace includes the office, a place visited because of the work, and transport provided for that work. It covers a woman of any age, whether or not she is on the rolls. Employee is defined widely and includes a trainee, an apprentice, a contract worker, and a volunteer. Where there are ten or more workers, the employer must constitute an Internal Committee. Where there are fewer than ten, the Local Committee is the forum.

Why everyone should take it

Any person at the workplace may meet this conduct, witness it, or be asked about it. The training sets out what unwelcome conduct is, what may amount to sexual harassment and what may not, how a complaint is made, what the Internal Committee does, and what must stay confidential. A manager, a colleague, and a person who may support a complainant need the same understanding.
